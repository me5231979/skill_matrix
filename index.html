How it works

Three steps to a destination.

01

Start where you are

Select your current role. You'll see the six core competencies every Vanderbilt staff member shares, the skills the framework maps to your role, and — in dashed grey — AI-inferred skills you likely also carry.

02

Choose a destination

Select any role you're curious about, in any family. The matrix compares both skill sets: gold means it carries over, oak means a bridge exists, black outline means it's new ground.

03

Get the plan

A printable Learning & Development Plan: phase checklists, a skill development table with recommended learning (Oracle Learning deep links, named podcasts and certifications, YouTube, white papers), and a step-by-step Oracle playbook — Talent Profile, Oracle Grow, and Oracle Learning.
