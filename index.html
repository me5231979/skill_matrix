          A note on outcomes

          Growth is guaranteed. Placement is not.

          This tool and the plans it builds are development roadmaps, not promises of placement. Completing a plan, every course, goal and gig, builds real readiness, but it does not guarantee selection for, or transfer into, any role. Internal openings are filled through Vanderbilt’s standard recruitment process, and selection depends on position availability, business needs, qualifications and the strength of the applicant pool.

          What the work does guarantee: the skills are yours. They strengthen your performance in your current role, enrich your Talent Profile and make you a stronger candidate whenever the right opening appears.

          What good looks like

          A résumé in skills language

          A fictitious example: Dining Services → IT support pathway

          Before

          “Worked in the dining hall for 8 years. Duties included cooking, cleaning and inventory.”

          After: lead with skills, prove them with results

          • Inventory Management Systems: ran daily counts and orders in a digital inventory system for a 3,000-meal operation, cutting waste 12%
          • Customer Support: resolved 30+ guest questions and issues per shift, keeping satisfaction scores above 90%
          • Process Improvement: redesigned the prep-line workflow, saving 45 staff minutes per day
          • Technology in daily work: first on the team to adopt the new point-of-sale and scheduling systems, then trained 12 coworkers

          Same job. Same person. The second version names the transferable skills a hiring manager in the destination role is scanning for, with evidence attached.
